I am a PhD Student in Computer Science at [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/), advised by Prof. [Hyesoon Kim](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/people/hyesoon-kim) in the [HPArch Lab](https://sites.gatech.edu/hparch/). My research lies at the intersection of compilers, computer architecture, and GPUs.

During my PhD I have spent three summers at [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/) working on GPU kernel performance analysis and performance modeling for LLM inference workloads in the Deep Learning Performance Architecture team.

Before starting my PhD I worked as a C++/LLVM software engineer at [Arm](https://www.arm.com/) in the Fast Models team, building GPU functional models of Mali GPUs.
